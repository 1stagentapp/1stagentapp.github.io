Privacy policy

How 1st Agent App handles personal data across our website, hosted onboarding agents, APIs and open-source community — including AI processing, subprocessors, international transfers, retention and your rights.

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This page is maintained by 1st Agent App to answer common privacy and data-handling questions about the platform. It describes our own practices, is not independent verification or certification, and is not legal advice for your business — consult your counsel about your obligations.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how 1st Agent App, a Delaware limited liability company with a principal place of business in the State of Delaware, USA ("1st Agent App", "we", "us"), collects, uses, shares and protects personal data. It covers our websites, documentation, community and public repositories, and our hosted onboarding agent platform and APIs (together, the "Service").

When you use the Service as an individual or sign up directly, we act as a controller (a "business" under US state privacy laws). When our customer — your employer or the organization that deployed an onboarding agent — configures the Service and uploads content, that customer is the controller and we act as its processor ("service provider"), handling data on its instructions. If you are an end user of a customer's agent, please direct requests to that organization first; we will assist them.

Our open-source repositories are also subject to the privacy practices of the platform hosting them (for example GitHub), and any public contribution you make — commits, issues, comments — is visible indefinitely.

2. Personal data we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we collect:

  • Account and contact data: name, work email, employer, job title, password hashes or federated identity identifiers, and role or permission assignments.
  • Billing data: billing contact, address, tax identifiers, plan and invoice history. Card details are collected and stored by our payment processor, not by us.
  • Customer content: policies, procedures, documents, media and configuration that a customer uploads so the agent can be trained on that organization's processes.
  • Conversation data: text, audio and video an end user provides to an agent, transcripts, generated agent responses, uploaded verification artifacts, and step-completion records.
  • Usage and device data: IP address, browser and device characteristics, timestamps, pages and features used, referral data, error and performance diagnostics, and API and agent-minute metering.
  • Support and communications: messages, demo and contact-form submissions, and records of correspondence with our team.
  • Community data: public repository activity, issues, pull requests, and contributor identity where you choose to share it.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: strictly necessary session and security cookies, plus preference and limited analytics identifiers where permitted.

3. Where the data comes from

We collect data directly from you, automatically from your use of the Service, from the customer that deployed the agent you interact with, and from integrations you or your administrator connect (for example identity providers, HR and learning systems, ticketing tools and payment processors). We may also receive limited business-contact data from public sources and marketing partners for outreach, where lawful.

4. Sensitive data and children

The Service is not designed as a repository for special-category or sensitive personal data. Do not upload government identifiers, health, biometric, precise geolocation, financial account, or similar sensitive data unless a written agreement with us expressly permits it. Where a customer's onboarding flow involves voice or video, the customer is responsible for any notices and consents required, including under biometric privacy laws.

The Service is intended for business use by individuals 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child's data has been submitted, contact privacy@1stagent.app and we will delete it.

5. How and why we use personal data

We use personal data to:

  • provide, operate, personalize and secure the Service, including running onboarding agent sessions and recording progress;
  • authenticate users, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our terms, and maintain audit and security logs;
  • process payments, manage subscriptions, invoicing and collections;
  • provide support, respond to inquiries, and send service and security notices;
  • monitor reliability, debug, and improve quality, safety and accuracy of our models and product using aggregated or de-identified data wherever practicable;
  • send marketing communications about our products, with opt-out in every message and consent where required;
  • comply with law, respond to lawful requests, and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies and we act as controller, we rely on: performance of a contract (providing and billing for the Service); our legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, product improvement, business-to-business marketing), balanced against your rights; your consent (non-essential cookies, certain marketing, optional processing) which you may withdraw at any time; and compliance with legal obligations.

When we act as processor for a customer, that customer determines the legal basis for its processing.

7. AI and automated processing

The Service uses machine-learning models, including models operated by third-party providers under contract, to interpret input and generate agent responses. Prompts and content sent to those providers are subject to contractual restrictions prohibiting their use to train the providers' general-purpose models.

We do not use customer content or conversation data to train foundation models for the benefit of other customers unless the customer opts in in writing. We may use aggregated, de-identified or synthetic data — which cannot reasonably be re-associated with an individual — to evaluate and improve quality and safety.

Agent output can be inaccurate. The Service is not designed to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about an individual without human involvement, and customers must not configure it to do so. Where a customer nonetheless uses agent output in a consequential decision, that customer is responsible for providing meaningful human review and any required disclosures. You may ask us for information about the logic involved, and object to processing, as described in section 12.

8. We do not sell your personal data

We do not sell personal data for money, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, or comparable US state laws. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes beyond those permitted by those laws.

9. Disclosure and subprocessors

We disclose personal data only as follows:

  • Service providers and subprocessors that host infrastructure, deliver email, process payments, provide model inference, analytics, error monitoring and customer support, each under a written contract limiting use to our instructions. A current subprocessor list is available on request or in our security documentation.
  • The customer organization that deployed the agent, including its administrators, who can see onboarding progress and conversation records.
  • Integrations you or your administrator enable, which receive only the data needed for that integration to function.
  • Legal and safety recipients: to comply with law, valid legal process or government requests, and to protect the rights, safety and property of users, the public or us. We will seek to notify affected customers of compulsory disclosure unless legally prohibited.
  • Corporate transactions: to a counterparty in a merger, acquisition, financing or asset sale, subject to this policy continuing to apply to the transferred data.
  • With your consent, or as otherwise directed by you.

10. International transfers

We are based in the United States and use infrastructure and subprocessors that may process data in the US and other countries. Where we transfer personal data from the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we rely on appropriate safeguards, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, together with supplementary technical and organizational measures. Copies of the relevant transfer mechanism are available on request, and regional data residency may be available on eligible plans.

11. Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described here. Account and billing records are kept for the life of the account plus the period required by tax and corporate law. Customer content and conversation data are retained for the period the customer configures, and deleted on the customer's request or at contract end; we make content available for export for 30 days after termination.

Security, audit and metering logs are typically retained for up to 12 months. Backups are rotated on a defined schedule, so deleted data may persist briefly in encrypted backups before being overwritten. We may retain data longer where required for legal holds, dispute resolution or compliance, and de-identified or aggregated data indefinitely.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of your personal data; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent; to opt out of targeted advertising, sale or profiling (which we do not conduct); to appeal a denied request; and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To exercise a right, email privacy@1stagent.app from the address associated with your data, or use the controls in your account. We will verify your identity proportionately, respond within the time required by applicable law (generally 30 days, extendable where permitted), and honour authorized agents where the law requires. If we act as a processor, we will forward your request to the relevant customer and support their response.

EEA, UK and Swiss residents may lodge a complaint with their supervisory authority; California residents may contact the California Privacy Protection Agency. Under Nevada and similar state laws, we honour opt-out requests for any covered sale, which we do not conduct.

13. Cookies, analytics and tracking preferences

We use strictly necessary cookies for authentication, load balancing and security, and, where permitted, preference and analytics cookies to understand aggregate product usage. Where consent is required, we request it before setting non-essential cookies and you can change your choice at any time.

You can also control cookies in your browser; blocking necessary cookies may break sign-in. We honour Global Privacy Control and similar browser opt-out signals where technically feasible.

14. Security

We maintain administrative, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the Service, including encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access control and least-privilege internal access, tenant isolation, secrets management, logging and monitoring, dependency and vulnerability scanning, code review, and vendor due diligence. Our practices are described further on our Security page.

No system is perfectly secure. You are responsible for protecting your credentials, enabling available multi-factor authentication, and managing which of your users have access. If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting your data, we will notify affected customers without undue delay and as required by applicable law, and cooperate on remediation. Report suspected vulnerabilities to security@1stagent.app.

15. Open-source and community privacy

Our open-source components can be self-hosted. When you self-host, you are the controller of the data you process and this policy does not apply to that deployment; we receive no data from it unless you enable optional telemetry or connect to our hosted APIs.

Contributions, issues and discussions in our public repositories are public by design, and the name and email in your commit metadata become part of the permanent project history. Do not include personal or confidential data in public issues, logs or sample data.

16. Marketing and communications

We send service, billing and security notices as part of the Service; these cannot be opted out of while your account is active. Marketing email includes an unsubscribe link and we honour it promptly. We use consent-based marketing where required, and we do not send SMS marketing without express opt-in.

17. Third-party links and services

The Service links to and integrates with third-party products, including identity providers, HR systems, model providers, payment processors and community platforms. Their privacy practices are their own; review their notices before sharing data with them.

18. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service, our vendors or the law changes. We will update the date below and, for material changes, notify you by email or in-product before the change takes effect. Continued use after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.

19. How to contact us

Privacy questions, requests and complaints: privacy@1stagent.app. Legal notices: legal@1stagent.app. Security reports: security@1stagent.app. Postal mail: 1st Agent App, Privacy Team, State of Delaware, USA — email us for the current registered address for formal service.

Customers requiring a data processing addendum, subprocessor list, standard contractual clauses or a security review package can request them at privacy@1stagent.app.

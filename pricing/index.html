Before you ask

Why price per program instead of per seat?+

Onboarding is bursty. Seats punish you for hiring, so we charge for the programs you actually run — hire 5 people or 500 in a month and the price is identical.

Is the open-source version crippled?+

No. The agent, grounded answers and verification all ship in the open-source build. Cloud plans add managed hosting, integrations, analytics and support.

What happens after the demo?+

You get a written rollout plan and a fixed quote. No pressure, no auto-renewing trials, and you can cancel a cloud plan at the end of any term.

Do you charge for model usage?+

Cloud plans include a generous model allowance covering typical onboarding volume. Heavy usage is billed at cost with no markup, shown in your dashboard.

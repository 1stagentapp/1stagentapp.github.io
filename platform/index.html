What's inside

Grounded knowledge

Upload policies, handbooks and process docs. The agent answers only from approved sources, with citations on every response.

Video presence

A consistent human-grade presenter that holds attention far better than a static checklist or a 40-slide deck.

Step verification

Nothing is marked complete until it is actually done — confirmed through integrations, not self-reporting.

Program analytics

See drop-off by step, recurring questions and which policies need clearer language.

Works with your stack

HRIS, identity, ticketing and LMS connections keep the agent in sync with the systems you already run.

Open by default
